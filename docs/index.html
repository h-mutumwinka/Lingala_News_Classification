About the model

Fine-tuned AfroXLMR-base (Alabi et al., 2022) on the Lingala part of MasakhaNEWS (Adelani et al., 2023): 608 Voice of America Lingala articles. Test macro-F1 0.911, accuracy 93%. The model runs entirely in your browser with Transformers.js (8-bit ONNX version of Heroine2/lingala-news-topic-classifier); your text is not sent to any server. Code and report: GitHub.

Limitations. Only four topics are known, so an article about culture or religion is still forced into one of them. The training news is from VOA Lingala, mostly 2018-2023 (Ebola, COVID-19, DRC politics); other styles may be classified less reliably. Articles that mix topics, such as a parliament vote on a health emergency, are the most common errors.
